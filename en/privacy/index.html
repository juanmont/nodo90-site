1. Controller

Nodo90 is an independent project run by [MI NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS REALES], an individual. Contact: nodo90tech@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

Nodo90 only processes the data needed to connect a social account and publish content to it:

  • Connected account data, which we receive from the platform through OAuth after the account holder's authorisation: the account or profile identifier, username and display name, and the permissions (scopes) granted.
  • OAuth credentials (access and refresh tokens), needed to publish on behalf of the account. They are managed by Postiz, the open-source software we use to connect to social media, and are stored in its own database. They are not stored in the project's code repository or in Nodo90's own database.
  • Content and publication metadata: the video, title, description, tags and publication settings (for example, privacy).
  • Technical and publication records: the record of whether each publication was requested, sent and with what result, and technical operating logs.

We do not receive the password of any platform. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising or profiling. This informational website does not use its own cookies or analytics tools.

3. Purposes

To authenticate the connection to the account, prepare the content, publish it, record the result, and maintain the security and traceability of the service. We do not use the data for any other purpose.

4. Legal basis

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process data on the following bases:

  • Providing the requested service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): connecting an account through OAuth and preparing and publishing content when the user requests or configures it.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): technically necessary security, traceability, duplicate-prevention and audit processing, to maintain the integrity of the service and prevent duplicate or unauthorised publications, always balanced against the rights of the people concerned.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): where a rule requires us to retain certain data.

If a specific processing activity were based on consent, it may be withdrawn at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. Providers and third parties

  • Postiz: open-source software that we run ourselves. It manages the connection to social media and holds the OAuth credentials.
  • Cloudflare R2: video files may be stored temporarily in Cloudflare R2 and made available through a technical URL so that the destination social platform can download them during the publication process.
  • Supabase: the project's database (content, metadata and publication records). It does not contain social credentials.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of this informational website. As a hosting provider, GitHub may process technical visit data (for example, the IP address) under its own policy.
  • Connected social platforms (for example, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube): they receive the content and settings published to the connected account and process them as independent controllers, under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. International transfers

Certain providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where applicable, that processing is carried out under the corresponding legal mechanisms. We do not give specific guarantees about the particular mechanism used by each provider beyond what their own terms establish.

7. Retention

  • Credentials: while the integration is active or until it is revoked or becomes invalid.
  • Content and metadata: as long as needed to prepare or publish the content, or until deleted where appropriate.
  • Publication records and technical logs: for the period reasonably necessary for security, traceability, duplicate prevention and audit.

8. Revocation and disconnection

The account holder can withdraw permission at any time from the security or connected-apps settings of the platform itself (on TikTok: account settings, security and app permissions), or by asking us at nodo90tech@gmail.com.

When an integration is revoked or disconnected, Nodo90 stops using its credentials to access the platform. Where the platform allows it, the authorisation can be revoked or invalidated. Certain technical or publication records may be kept for the time necessary for security, traceability, duplicate prevention, audit or compliance with legal obligations.

9. Rights

Users in the European Union and Spain may exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability (where applicable), and may withdraw consent where consent is the basis of the processing, by writing to nodo90tech@gmail.com. They may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority; in Spain, the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es). If you live in another country, you may contact the data protection authority of your own.
