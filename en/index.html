Complex things, made clear in under 90 seconds.

Nodo90 is a platform to prepare and publish short educational videos on social media.

What Nodo90 is

Nodo90 is an independent project dedicated to explaining complex topics clearly in videos under 90 seconds long. The platform helps prepare that short content (script, video, title, description and tags) and connect it to social media for publication.

Connection to social media

Nodo90 connects to social platforms through their official APIs. The person who manages an account explicitly authorises it through each platform's own sign-in (OAuth) and can revoke that permission at any time. Nodo90 only acts on accounts authorised this way.

Project status

Nodo90 is under development. There is no public user registration yet.
